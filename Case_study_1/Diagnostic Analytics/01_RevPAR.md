# Why Did RevPAR Increase Only Slightly in 2025?

Based on the descriptive analysis, RevPAR increased from **$15.47 in 2023** to **$16.11 in 2024 (+4.17%)**, before slowing to **$16.22 in 2025 (+0.68%)**.

| Year | ADR    | RevPAR | Occupancy |
| ---- | ------ | ------ | --------- |
| 2023 | $22.56 | $15.47 | 69%       |
| 2024 | $23.49 | $16.11 | 69%       |
| 2025 | $23.63 | $16.22 | 69%       |

Since:

RevPAR = ADR × Occupancy

I investigated both components separately to determine the primary driver of RevPAR performance.

## 1. Occupancy Remained Flat

Occupancy remained stable at approximately **69% across all three years**.

This indicates that demand neither increased nor decreased significantly during the analysis period. The hotels sold a similar proportion of available rooms each year, meaning occupancy was not the primary driver of RevPAR growth.

**Key Insight:**  
Demand remained stable, eliminating occupancy as the main factor behind the change in RevPAR.

---

### 2. ADR Growth Slowed Significantly

ADR increased as follows:

- **$22.56 (2023)** → **$23.49 (2024)** (**+4.12%**)
- **$23.49 (2024)** → **$23.63 (2025)** (**+0.61%**)

The ADR growth rate in 2025 closely mirrors the RevPAR growth rate of **0.68%**, suggesting that RevPAR performance was almost entirely driven by pricing rather than demand.

**Key Insight:**  
The slowdown in ADR growth directly translated into slower RevPAR growth.

---

### 3. Channel Analysis Confirmed Pricing Stagnation

Further analysis of booking channels showed that ADR remained relatively stable between 2024 and 2025.

| Booking Channel   | 2024 ADR | 2025 ADR |
| ----------------- | -------- | -------- |
| Velora.com        | 385.76   | 382.28   |
| OTA - Booking.com | 386.39   | 383.18   |
| Property Direct   | 384.04   | 381.71   |
| Travel Agent      | 381.44   | 386.35   |

While some channels experienced minor ADR growth, many channels saw only marginal improvements or slight declines. This limited pricing growth prevented a larger increase in overall ADR and consequently constrained RevPAR growth.

**Key Insight:**  
The majority of booking channels showed little pricing expansion between 2024 and 2025, limiting revenue growth opportunities.

---

## Business Insight

The RevPAR slowdown in 2025 was **not a demand issue**, as occupancy remained stable at 69%. Instead, it was primarily a **pricing issue**.

Following a strong ADR increase in 2024, pricing growth plateaued in 2025 across most booking channels. Because occupancy remained unchanged, ADR became the only meaningful lever for RevPAR growth. The limited ADR increase of **0.61%** directly restricted RevPAR growth to **0.68%**.

---

## Executive Summary

RevPAR growth decelerated from **4.17% in 2024** to **0.68% in 2025** because occupancy remained unchanged at **69%**, leaving ADR as the primary growth driver. Analysis of booking channel performance revealed minimal ADR improvement between 2024 and 2025, indicating pricing stagnation rather than demand weakness. Therefore, the slowdown in RevPAR was primarily driven by limited rate growth rather than a decline in occupancy.
